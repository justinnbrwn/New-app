HashMargin Pro — Free Calculator Beta Terms
Last updated: October 26, 2026

HashMargin Pro's public beta is an illustrative mining-economics calculator. Figures are estimates based on inputs supplied by the visitor; they are not live pool measurements, confirmed payouts, or guaranteed financial results.

The beta does not connect to, monitor, operate, or control mining hardware. Worker-offline notifications, subscriptions, account management, and paid services are not available in this public preview.

Use the calculator for informational planning only, and independently verify electricity rates, pool fees, mining returns, equipment performance, and other costs. No investment or financial advice is provided.

Do not submit passwords, wallet secrets, or API credentials.

This is a provisional beta notice, not a substitute for legal review before commercial launch. A verified support contact, jurisdiction-specific terms, and privacy review should be completed before offering paid service.
