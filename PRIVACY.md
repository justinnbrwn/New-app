HashMargin Pro — Calculator Beta and Account Testing Privacy Notice
Operator: Justinn Brown
Last updated: October 27, 2026

Calculator inputs are processed in your browser. They are not intentionally sent to HashMargin's server. No mining hardware or pool connection is available.

Account testing is disabled until explicitly configured. When enabled, the email you supply and one-time sign-in code or copied email confirmation link are sent through HashMargin to Supabase Auth to create or authenticate your account. Supabase manages identity verification, code expiry and account records. HashMargin does not store account passwords. Your browser keeps its short-lived access token only in memory; refreshing or closing the page clears it locally. Signing out requests server sign-out too. HashMargin does not return refresh tokens to the browser.

When subscription testing is configured, Stripe hosts test checkout and subscription management. Stripe receives your test checkout information. HashMargin stores test customer and subscription identifiers linked to your verified account in Supabase, and checks subscription status with Stripe. Do not enter real card details in this test environment. Live payments are disabled.

Render and these providers may process technical access and security information, including IP addresses. Application logging omits request bodies and authentication secrets. Provider retention policies also apply. Account and test billing records remain until removed by the operator or under the providers' retention policies; automated deletion is not yet implemented.

Do not submit wallet private keys, miner API tokens or sensitive information through the calculator. No commercial service is being offered. A verified support contact and account-deletion workflow must be available before wider account or paid-service launch.
